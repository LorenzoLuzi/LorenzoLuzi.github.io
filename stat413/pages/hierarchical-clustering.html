Hierarchical Clustering

Here is the algorithm for Hierarchical clustering:

  1. Each point is its own cluster
  2. For i = n, n-1, n-2, ..., 2:
    1. Compare all clusters to one another (dissimilarity metric)
    2. Fuse the two most similar ones, Dissimilarity tells you height in dendogram

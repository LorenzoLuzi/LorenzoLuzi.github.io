\( \gamma_{im} = \dfrac{\pi_m N(x_i;\, \mu_m, \sigma_m^2)}{\sum_{j=1}^{M} \pi_j N(x_i;\, \mu_j, \sigma_j^2)} \)
M-STEP — parameters from responsibilities:
\( N_m = \sum_i \gamma_{im}, \qquad \pi_m = \dfrac{N_m}{n}, \qquad \mu_m = \dfrac{1}{N_m} \sum_i \gamma_{im}\, x_i \)
\( \sigma_m^2 = \dfrac{1}{N_m} \sum_i \gamma_{im}\, (x_i - \mu_m)^2 \)
